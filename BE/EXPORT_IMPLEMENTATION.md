- All own-org owner/admin/staff may request ORDERS/REPORT_PRODUCTS. REPORT_BUYERS is vendor-only. Each artifact belongs permanently to its requesting account and original organization/country; colleagues and platform administrators cannot get/download/replay its identity. Current session/account/org authority applies on every HTTP operation and before worker publication. Logout alone does not discard an account-owned job; a later valid session can use it.

- Explicit inclusive country-local from/to dates, no future/reversed ranges, at most60 inclusive calendar months for each kind. Worker snapshot is taken at generation, not creation: later committed delivery may change a historical report before generation. One read-only REPEATABLE READ snapshot generates the entire file; current authorization guards remain held in a separate outer transaction, as for reporting. One nonblocking worker per instance, at most one job/five-second tick on a separate coordinator, existing10-connection pool,30s outer/25s snapshot/5s SQL/2s lock limits. Across instances the job row and current org/account guards serialize completion. No provider call or uncertain external outcome; crash rolls back to QUEUED. Credential-operation barriers defer, revoked account/org authority fails ACCESS_REVOKED.
- CSV uses UTF-8 without BOM, comma, CRLF rows, double quotes around every field and doubled embedded quotes. Null becomes an empty field. Prefix apostrophe when a field begins with control whitespace or its first non-whitespace/space/format character is =,+,-,@ or its full-width variant. All embedded separators/quotes stay inside their quoted field. This implements the generated-file prefix contract; no claim is made about a spreadsheet re-saving/re-opening or downstream transformation. [OWASP CSV injection guidance](https://community.owasp.org/attacks/CSV_Injection) reviewed2026-10-21 notes those application-dependent limits. No locale-dependent decimal, tax/payment or conversion semantics.
- At most100,000 data rows and16MiB UTF-8/file, never partial READY output. Whole result/count validation precedes publication. The byte cap bounds heap/temporary database growth for the pilot; this is an assistant operational choice. One worker generates at most one bounded file at a time. A reviewed128MiB global current-row artifact budget bounds transient PostgreSQL growth across instances/countries (technical bytes only, no commercial aggregation); a transaction advisory guard after the job lock serializes publication. If publishing would exceed it, fail safely with EXPORT_CAPACITY_REACHED and no bytes; users may explicitly request a new job after expiry/cleanup frees capacity. Cleanup only reduces usage and takes no storage guard.
