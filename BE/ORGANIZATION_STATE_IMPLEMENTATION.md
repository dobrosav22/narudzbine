# Organization suspension and reactivation

2026-10-21. Delegated refinement of DESIGN_SPEC organization states under D-GOV-005; assistant selection, not a new user answer. Backend/infrastructure only. Existing adminSuspendOrganization and adminReactivateOrganization contracts form this batch; closure is the next separate batch.

Only a currently authorized named platform administrator may change ACTIVE to SUSPENDED or SUSPENDED to ACTIVE. Require the current signed organization revision and a nonblank private reason (maximum1000 characters). CLOSED rejects either operation with409 ORGANIZATION_CLOSED. Current-revision same-state requests succeed without changes, audit or session revocation; stale revisions fail409 even when the requested state already holds. No separate retry key is introduced.

An actual suspension revokes every currently unrevoked session belonging to the organization's permanent business accounts. Account state, roles, security epochs, credential generations, pending identity/provider operations, invitations, ownership transfers and prepared work remain unchanged. Reactivation never revives a token; fresh ordinary login remains subject to all existing credential/current-membership checks. This selects revocation rather than temporary token dormancy so a previously lost token cannot regain access after reactivation. Suspension does not cancel orders, deduct/credit stock, disconnect relationships, change plan assignments or publish commercial notifications.

Lock the target organization first, then all its business account identities plus the actual administrator in UUID order, then authenticate the current administrator/session and check CSRF. Organization guards serialize business membership changes and login/session writes; the ordered account locks serialize credential/session workers. The final transaction writes state/revision/timestamp, session revocations and one private administrative audit atomically. Audit keeps the real platform actor, null original business scope, historical target organization/country and private reason; generic audit reads continue to omit that reason. No victim-attributed synthetic session audit is emitted. Existing device invalidation triggers follow session revocation.

Migration57 extends only the append-only audit action/resource constraint and schema marker. No historical event, organization identity or commercial row is rewritten. Default-off identity HTTP fencing remains. Verification must cover both countries/types, all sessions and fresh login, unchanged membership/credentials/history, same-state/stale behavior, CLOSED protection, current administrator revocation during a real lock wait, browser CSRF, atomic audit failure rollback and populated56 migration. Full wrapper, distinct review and changed artifact gates remain mandatory; evidence is recorded separately after execution.
