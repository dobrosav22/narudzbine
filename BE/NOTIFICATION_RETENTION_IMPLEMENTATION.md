# Notification content and metadata retention

Selected2026-10-21 under D-GOV-005/D-PLAN-018/R-PLAN-018 before dependent code. Backend/infrastructure only; routine technical TTL, not commercial or assisted legal deletion. Existing inbox list/mark-read already hide entries at ninety elapsed days. No REST/DTO change or provider activation.

Three independent ten-second REQUIRES_NEW maintenance jobs use an injected UTC millisecond Clock and at most1000 targets per run:

* **Inbox:** after ninety days from original creation, retire only terminal PUBLISHED/SUPPRESSED/EXPIRED work. Keep original work ID, source/recipient/scope/sequence/outcome/completion evidence; set an internal one-way retired flag and clear cached organization/order-reference text. Delete the matching published notification in the same transaction. Coordinate the deferred publication invariant and DELETE guard: a published unretired work item must have a notification; a retired item must not. Initial retirement, restoration, requeue, arbitrary source deletion or notification deletion without retirement remain forbidden. Lock published work/notification together with SKIP LOCKED, then use any remaining part of the1000 budget for terminal unpublished work. QUEUED work is left to the existing expiry/publication worker. No retained source can publish again, including Clock rollback.
* **Delivery metadata:** after ninety days from original terminal_at, select only definite DELIVERED/FAILED/SUPPRESSED/EXPIRED jobs with their rendered body already retired and no recently received matching callback. Keep immutable job/fanout/source/device/channel/creation/outcome/terminal identity, preventing a new send for the original source. One-way metadata retirement clears destination, provider reference, attempted device revision/token hash, attempt count and first/next-attempt timing. Nullable fields represent retired metadata, not zero attempts. Initial jobs remain nonretired with required attempt count/next time; existing active lease/fence/body and terminal guards remain. QUEUED/SENDING/ACCEPTED/UNKNOWN and recent callback evidence defer retirement. Processing a retired terminal job cannot call a provider or dereference retired counters.
* **Callback metadata:** after ninety days from receipt, clear provider reference/event type/receipt time behind a one-way marker, retaining immutable callback ID/payload digest for duplicate/conflicting-replay handling. Do not delete that identity or permit restoration/initial retired inserts. Associated unretired jobs defer cleanup; uncoupled EMAIL SENDING/ACCEPTED/UNKNOWN jobs without a provider reference conservatively defer callback metadata because association is not yet known. This can retain metadata beyond ninety days until actual reconciliation; do not invent an association/outcome to release it.

Delivery/callback cleanup acquires the same provider-reference advisory guard as trusted callbacks/result commits, nonblocking and before target row locks, then rechecks eligibility in a fresh statement. At most1000 ordered candidates are examined; row locks skip current writers. Recent callbacks and new provider-result association cannot be silently missed across the retirement decision. Callback bodies/signatures are not stored here. Retained ID/digest/fanout/job outcomes are minimal duplicate/reconciliation identities, not proof of legal compliance or permission for general history retention.
