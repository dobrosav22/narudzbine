## Implementation

_Originally `BE/PRICING_IMPLEMENTATION.md`: Base pricing and volume-tier implementation._

2026-10-19, assistant refinements under D-GOV-005, not user answers. Implements existing getPricing/updatePricing and the ProductUpdate.pricingCorrection branch for R-PRI-001/002/003/009/016/017/019/033/034/036. Actual activation, agreed prices/promotions, buyer offers/order reviews and submitted snapshots remain later integrations.

- Schema26 gives each genuine catalogue_product an empty pricing aggregate (id=productId, revision1, base=null, tiers=[]), including existing details; quota-only identities gain no invented details/prices. A database insertion trigger initializes future empty aggregates in the product creation transaction. No zero price/stock, free-plan inference or activation.
- Base/tier input is a strict positive decimal string, at most9 integer/two fractional digits, no whitespace/sign/exponent/leading zero. Normalize to two decimal places exactly, without rounding excess input precision. Output money strings have two decimals; currency comes from immutable vendor country (RSD/BAM), with no conversion/tax calculation.
- Tiers are a quantity-keyed schedule, accepted in any order and returned ascending. Reject duplicate thresholds, more than50, baseless tiers, nonpositive prices, nondecreasing prices, or thresholds outside1..1,000,000,000/not strictly above minimum/not minimum+n*step. Full replacement never silently drops tiers. Internal ordinary-price resolution selects the highest reached threshold for every unit, after quantity validation; it is not a buyer offer/order submission.
- Own-vendor active owner/admin/staff uses current EDIT_PRICES; browser writes retain CSRF/origin checks. Pricing revisions use pricing/v1 and productId, distinct from product/v1. Normalized no-op preserves revision/history/audit. Real pricing changes advance only pricing revision and retain immutable actual revision snapshots plus field-name audit in the same transaction.
- Catalogue exposes a lightweight product-pricing context and a quantity compatibility policy through catalogue.api. Pricing implements the policy; catalogue never imports pricing implementations. ProductService checks compatibility before quantity edits. Identity transport coordinates an explicit pricingCorrection under catalogue+price authority and both current revisions: validate/stage the complete proposed schedule against proposed quantity rules, then save product changes. Any reference/SKU/freeze/revision/audit/commit failure rolls everything back. Deferred database constraints validate final quantity/base/tier consistency at commit, including direct writers; no circular service dependency.
- GET has no mutation. Both price saves and product edits take the existing vendor organization guard, coordinating quantity/price edits, revocation and plan/reference mutations. Providers remain outside this path. Unchanged catalogue fields can accompany a pricing-only correction without advancing product revision; no-op correction needs both current revisions.
- deactivateProduct:true requires catalogue authority as well as price authority. Current products can only be INACTIVE/ARCHIVED, so it is an already-inactive no-op and never unarchives. Actual ACTIVE state/deactivation and active base-removal protection must be integrated before activation is enabled; this batch does not fabricate active products or claim R-PRI-020 end-to-end acceptance. Base=null requires tiers=[]; future agreed prices must be retained by their separate integration.

Runtime additionally needs SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE on product_pricing, SELECT/INSERT/DELETE on product_volume_tier, and SELECT/INSERT on product_pricing_history (no history UPDATE/DELETE or pricing-aggregate DELETE). Initialization trigger needs runtime pricing INSERT. Migration owner alone applies schema changes. History records actual successful changes with actor/clock/revision/base/tier snapshot; audit stores field names, not commercial payloads. No provider provisioning, frontend changes or production release.

Publication follow-up: activation/deactivation and combined pricing deactivation are subsequently delivered at schema31 in [activation implementation](../catalogue/activation.md#implementation)/[evidence](../catalogue/activation.md#verification). Earlier batch limitations above describe their original verification boundary. Buyer/order and external acceptance remain unfinished.
