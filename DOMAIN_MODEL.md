## Approved catalogue rules — D-CAT-001–047
- Each vendor-owned sellable item is an independent product/SKU. Different pack sizes are separate products; no parent/variant relationship or pack conversion is selected.
- Every product requires a SKU code unique within its vendor after trimming surrounding whitespace and ignoring letter case. Different vendors may reuse codes. Archived products keep their codes reserved; restore the original product instead of creating a new product with that code. Catalogue-authorized edits retain all previous codes reserved for the same product. Trimmed codes are 1–64 ASCII letters/digits or -, _, . and /, with no internal spaces. Only current codes are searchable; retired previous codes are reservation/history only, never lookup aliases or candidates for reactivation, even on the same product. Archive/restore of an unchanged current code remains allowed. D-CAT-055 permits cosmetic current-code capitalization edits without another reservation; precise trim/case storage, no-op audit and concurrency remain open.
- Categories are flat; each product has zero or one category and zero or more optional tags. D-CAT-029 supersedes duplicate-name D-CAT-014: category names and tag names are separately unique within a vendor after trimming and ignoring case. Different vendors may reuse names; a category and tag may share a name. References still use IDs. Block removal until all product/promotion references are explicitly removed by authorized actors; no implicit detach/merge/cascade. Rename, count/field limits and historical reference handling remain open.
- Order quantities are whole numbers. Minimum and step are positive whole numbers, both defaulting to 1. A quantity must equal minimum + n × step for an integer n >= 0. Reject fractions, under-minimum quantities and step mismatches without rounding. Vendors define their own units; no shared platform list is imposed. Units are reusable vendor-owned records referenced by products; reject foreign-vendor references. Owner/admin and staff with catalogue permission may manage units subject to lifecycle constraints. D-CAT-028 supersedes duplicate-name D-CAT-023: unit names are unique within a vendor after trimming and ignoring case; different vendors may reuse names. Use IDs, not names, for references. Freeze a unit's name once a product using it appears in a submitted order. Reject unit deletion while any product, including archived, references it; referenced units remain available, with no archive workflow selected. Unit field validation, pre-trigger edits, maxima, numeric representation and stock adjustment stale-write/retry semantics remain open; D-CAT-040–042 approve absolute/delta nonnegative adjustments and optional-reason audit.
- A product's selling unit and pack size lock after its first submitted order. A semantic change requires a new product/SKU; changing its code alone does not bypass the lock. Pack contents are optional descriptive text, with no parsing for calculation/conversion. The entire pack text freezes on first submitted order, including absent text; no spelling-correction, addition or removal exception. Exact transaction/normalization rules and before-first-order edits remain open.
- Inactive and archived products cannot enter new orders, including reordered orders; historical orders remain readable. This does not automatically cancel existing orders or select historical snapshot fields.
- Catalogue-authorized creation may produce an incomplete, non-orderable product missing price and/or unit, or missing a stock count while vendor tracking is on. Missing price is distinct from zero. Price completion requires price authority and never implicitly activates the product. Explicit activation requires catalogue authority, valid pricing and a valid vendor-owned unit; a combined price/activation action requires both permissions. While tracking is on, a new incomplete product must receive a count from a stock-authorized actor before activation; supplying stock requires its separate grant.
- Catalogue-authorized restoration preserves the original product/SKU and requires capacity. Restored products are inactive; eligibility and explicit activation are required before ordering. Restore does not confer price/stock editing authority.
- All non-archived products, including inactive and unpriced/incomplete products, consume product capacity. SKU uniqueness and create/restore capacity must hold under concurrency.
- Exact database schema/enums, additional required fields, price validation beyond D-PRI-002/009–011, retained-price behavior after base removal under D-PRI-020, restore retention and transaction mechanics remain open. D-CAT-030–034 establish one vendor/product stock scope, whole sellable-unit counts, a vendor-wide tracking switch defaulting off, retained disabled counts requiring authorized reconciliation before re-enable, and blocked submissions on tracked shortages. Stock edits/toggling use stock authority. No per-product switch or per-warehouse allocation selected. D-CAT-035 initializes missing counts to zero at vendor enablement while retained counts require confirmation; this does not activate incomplete products. D-CAT-036 keeps new products created without stock while tracking is on non-orderable until stock-authorized completion and explicit activation.
- Under D-CAT-037–039, successful tracked submission immediately deducts from one available-to-order count. Unsubmitted carts/drafts hold no stock; submission validates current availability. No physical/reserved split is selected and dispatch does not deduct again. Valid cancellation before any dispatch restores that order's deduction exactly once; an order with no deduction cannot generate a credit. D-CAT-043 permits immediate tracking disable even with pending deducted orders, retaining history. D-CAT-045 requires immediate exactly-once cancellation credits to retained counts while off, without enabling tracking. D-CAT-046 permits re-enable with older orders after confirming available counts net of all their commitments, preserving original deduction/credit obligations across reconciliation. Off-mode orders receive no retroactive deduction or automatic credit; later cancellation may require an authorized manual adjustment. Partial dispatch is excluded by D-ORD-005. D-ORD-004/011–015 settle cancellation timing, vendor rights, rejection credits and launch return exclusion; D-ORD-021/022 settle buyer cancellation authority/withdrawal; transaction rules remain open.
- D-CAT-040–042 allow stock-authorized absolute available-count writes and whole-unit deltas. Reject any operation resulting below zero without clamping. Record actor/time/product/before-after for manual changes, including initial counts/reconciliation, with optional reason; automatic movements link to their order. D-CAT-047 allows these edits while tracking is off without enabling tracking or waiving re-enable confirmation. Upper bounds, event fields/retention and retry/stale-write remain open.
- D-CAT-044 preserves count/history on product archive. When vendor tracking is on, a stock-authorized actor must confirm/update stock before reactivation; restoration remains capacity-checked/inactive and activation still requires catalogue authority. Confirmation freshness and pending-order/archive interactions remain open. D-CAT-045–047 settle disabled/reconciled-count accounting; confirmation freshness and concurrency remain open.
